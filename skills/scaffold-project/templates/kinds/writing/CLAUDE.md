## Writing

**Writing vault:** {{writing_vault}}

- **The story lives in `{{writing_vault}}/`**, an Obsidian vault of its own. Wikilinks are fine inside it. From `context/`, link to it with relative Markdown links only; nothing in `{{writing_vault}}/` links into `context/`.
- **Prose is the author's.** Draft or revise story text only when asked, and only the passage asked about. Begin each drafted passage with `%% draft: agent %%` so it can be found and rewritten; Obsidian hides the comment, and word counts can exclude it. Otherwise, advise: notes, questions and continuity flags, never silent edits.
- **Canon** is what the written scenes and the character and world notes say. When new work contradicts it, report the contradiction; don't resolve it by editing either side.
- **Don't rename, move or delete** notes in `{{writing_vault}}/` from the shell. Obsidian updates every link to a note it renames; a shell rename breaks them. Ask the author to do it in Obsidian.
