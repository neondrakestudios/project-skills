## CAD project

- Every dimension carries its unit, and is marked as measured, designed or assumed when that isn't obvious.
- Never state a load, span or fastener capacity as fact without a cited source or a shown calculation; otherwise mark it unverified. A structural change is a cross-cutting decision.
- `context/MATERIALS.md` is the current bill of materials. Update it in the same {{change_unit}} as a design change that alters a part.
